Delete your Daily account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent.

From the app

  1. Open Daily and go to the Account page.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted right away and you are signed out.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email us from the address you used for your account:

daily@vyrdlabs.com with the subject Delete my Daily account.

We use the sending address to confirm the account is yours, and may reply to verify before acting. We will email you once the account has been deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details, including your email address.
  • Your medication list, schedules and reminders.
  • Your dose log and history.
  • Sharing: anyone you invited loses access to your records, and you lose access to records others shared with you.

Data that only lives on your phone is removed when you uninstall Daily.

This cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted we cannot recover your medication history or shared records. Export or note anything you want to keep first.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited time before they are purged. They are not restored except for security, disaster recovery or legal compliance.
  • Purchase and invoice records for Daily Plus are kept for up to 10 years, as tax and accounting law requires. They do not include your medication data.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 24 months after the conversation closes.

The retention section of the privacy policy has the full list.

Daily Plus subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Daily Plus subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store, otherwise you may keep being billed.

Questions

Email daily@vyrdlabs.com. Your rights over your data are described in the privacy policy.
