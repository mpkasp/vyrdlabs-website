Delete your Daily account

Deleting your account permanently removes it and all data associated with it. This cannot be undone.

In the app

On your iPhone, iPad or Android phone, open Daily and tap Profile, then the first row (your account), then Account. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also revokes Daily's Sign in with Apple access.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email daily@vyrdlabs.com from the address on your account. We may reply to verify it is you, and will confirm once the account has been deleted.

What is kept

Records we are legally required to keep, and for how long, are listed in the privacy policy. Deleting your account does not cancel a Daily Plus subscription. Cancel it where you bought it: on iPhone or iPad, in Settings > your name > Subscriptions; on Android, in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. See the terms.
