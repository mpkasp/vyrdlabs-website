Delete your Daily account

Daily is made by Vyrd Labs LLC. You can delete your Daily account and its data in the app, or ask us to do it by email.

In the app

  1. Open Daily and make sure you're connected to the internet.
  2. Go to Profile → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  4. Sign in with Google again when asked, choosing the same account.

Your account is deleted right away.

By email, if you no longer have the app

Email daily@vyrdlabs.com with the subject "Delete my Daily account", from the Google email address you signed in with. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you, then delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

Deleting is permanent and can't be undone. We delete, from our servers:

  • your account and sign-in;
  • your medicines, schedules, reminder settings and dose history;
  • children that only you look after, and all of their records;
  • your sharing connections and invites, so people who could see your records lose access, and you lose access to records others shared with you;
  • your subscription record at our billing provider, RevenueCat.

When you delete in the app, the records on that phone are deleted too.

What is kept

  • Children you look after with someone else stay with the other parent or caretaker, with their records.
  • Records on other phones. Another phone signed in to the account is signed out, but the records stored on it remain until you uninstall Daily or clear its storage on that phone.
  • Google Play purchase records, which Google keeps under its own policies.

We don't keep backups of deleted data.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Daily Plus. To stop being billed, cancel your subscription in Google Play subscriptions.

Just want Daily off one phone?

Uninstall Daily or clear its storage in your phone's settings. If several accounts use Daily on the phone, you can remove just one under Profile → Account → Other accounts on this phone. Your account and cloud data are kept.
