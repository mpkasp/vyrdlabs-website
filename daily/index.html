Daily: Medicine Reminder & Log

Pill and medicine reminders and a dose log for you and the people you care for.

Daily is a simple medicine reminder and dose log for the pills, vitamins and supplements you take, and for the people you care for. Add each medicine with its schedule, get a reminder when a dose is due, and log it, skip it or snooze it straight from the notification.

Everything lives on your phone and works offline. Daily never needs an account to remind you or keep your history, there is no limit on how many medicines you add, and there are no ads.

A pill reminder that fits your day

  • Choose exact times, certain days of the week, every few hours or every few days.
  • Add pills, drops, inhalers, injections and more, each with its strength and dose.
  • Keep as-needed medicines with no schedule and log a dose whenever you take one.
  • Repeat a reminder you have not answered.
  • Keep the medicine name out of the notification text.
  • Let a reminder through Do Not Disturb when you want it to.

A medication tracker you can look back on

  • Today shows what is due, what is coming up and what is done.
  • Every medicine keeps its own record: how many doses you logged over three months, six months or a year, and a history of each dose with the time it was logged.
  • Count the supply you have on hand and get a refill reminder before it runs low.
  • Archive a medicine when a course ends. Its history stays.

For families and caregivers

Keep the medicines of anyone you look after, such as a child, a partner or a parent, on your phone alongside your own. Each person has their own list, and Today shows whose dose is whose.

With Daily Plus, share a medication list with another caregiver, such as a co-parent, a sibling or a home helper, so you both see the same doses on your own phones. Was the morning dose given? The shared log shows who logged it and when.

Daily Plus also backs up your history and keeps it in step across your phones, so it follows you to a new one. It is an optional subscription with a free trial, and one plan covers everyone you invite.

Daily is a reminder and a log. It does not give medical advice, check interactions or suggest doses. Follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist for what to take and when.
