Privacy policy

Effective September 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Daily app collects, where it is kept, who can see it, and how to delete it. Daily is made by Vyrd Labs LLC ("we", "us"), 82 Wendell Ave. Ste 100, Pittsfield, MA 01201, USA.

The short version

  • Daily works without an account. Until you sign in, your medicines and dose history stay on your phone.
  • Your records reach our servers only when you use Daily Plus sync, share them with someone, or add a person to care for who is synced.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

What Daily collects

Health information you enter

The medicines you add (name, form, strength, color and shape), their schedules and dosages, reminder settings, and the doses you log, skip or snooze, with the time of each. This is health information, and we treat it that way.

Information about other people

Daily is built for shared care, so you may enter information about someone else: a child you look after (the name you give them and their medication records), or an adult who shares their medication records with you. When someone shares with you or you share with them, each of you sees the other's name and email address, and the dose log records who logged each dose. Only enter information about another person if you are their parent or guardian, or they have agreed to it.

Account information

Signing in is optional and is needed only to buy or restore Daily Plus, or to share records. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile photo address and a user ID. A child you add gets its own account ID, with no email or sign-in of its own.

Purchase information

Daily Plus is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your card or bank details. We receive your subscription status (for example, trial, active or expired) and the product you bought, linked to your user ID.

Technical information

When the app talks to our service providers, they receive what any internet connection carries, such as your IP address, device and operating system type, and app version. They use this to run the service, keep it secure and diagnose errors.

What Daily does not collect

Daily does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or browsing activity, and it contains no advertising or analytics software.

Where your information is kept

On your phone. Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, so Daily works offline. Daily does not add its own encryption to this storage; it relies on your phone's lock screen and the operating system's protections. If your phone's own backup is turned on (for example, Android backup to your Google account), that backup may include Daily's data.

In the cloud. Your records are copied to our servers only when:

  • you have Daily Plus and are signed in, to sync between your phones;
  • you share your records with another person, or they share theirs with you; or
  • you add a child whose records are synced under a Daily Plus plan.

Cloud data is stored with Google Firebase on Google Cloud, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and may be processed in the United States or other countries where Google operates.

How we use your information

  • To run Daily: show your medicines, schedule reminders, and keep your records in sync.
  • To let the people you choose see and log doses for the records you share.
  • To provide Daily Plus: check your subscription and free-trial eligibility.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We don't use your health information for advertising, sell it, or use it to build profiles about you.

Who we share it with

People you choose. When you share your records, the person you approve can see your medicines, schedules and dose history, and, if you allow it, log doses for you. A child's records are visible to each parent or caretaker who looks after them. You can stop sharing at any time in the app.

Children

Daily is for adults. Children do not use the app or have their own sign-in; a parent or guardian can add a child and track the child's medication. We do not knowingly collect information from children directly. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your cloud data for as long as your account exists. You can delete it at any time.

Delete your account

In Daily, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes, from our servers and from that phone:

  • your account, medicines, schedules and dose history;
  • children that only you look after, and their records (a child you look after with someone else stays with them);
  • your sharing connections, so people who could see your records lose access;
  • your subscription record at RevenueCat.

If you no longer have the app, see how to request deletion by email.

Some things to know:

  • Your subscription keeps billing until you cancel it in Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Other phones keep their copy. Another phone signed in to the account is signed out, but the records stored on it stay there until you uninstall Daily or clear its storage on that phone.
  • For up to an hour after deletion, a phone that was still signed in to the account can upload changes it had not yet sent. If that happens, contact us and we will delete them.
  • We do not keep separate backups of the cloud database, so deleted data is not held anywhere else by us. Copies in your own phone backups are controlled by you.

Signing out and removing Daily from a phone

Signing out keeps your records on the phone so they are there when you sign back in. To remove them from one phone without deleting your account, uninstall Daily or clear its storage. When several accounts use Daily on the same phone, you can remove one under Profile → Account → Other accounts on this phone. Either way, your account and cloud data are untouched.

Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit, and cloud data is encrypted at rest. Access rules limit each account's records to that account and the people it shares with. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your information in the app. You can also ask us to confirm what we hold, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, or stop processing it, by emailing daily@vyrdlabs.com from the email address on your account. We respond within 30 days, may need to confirm your identity first, and will not treat you differently for asking. Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or a US state with a privacy or consumer health data law), you may have further rights, including to withdraw consent and to complain to your local data protection authority. We honor these requests wherever you live.

We process your information to provide the service you asked for, with your consent for health information you choose to enter and share, and to meet legal obligations.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will let you know before they take effect, in the app or by email.
